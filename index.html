I work on machine learning for time series and applied mathematics, mostly forecasting under heavy noise and the statistical modeling that makes it tractable.

I am currently a research intern at the McGill Networks Research Lab with Prof. Mark Coates, studying how sequence models behave when the signal-to-noise ratio is low, using cross-sectional equity returns as the testbed. Before that I built a simulation framework for human–robot interaction at the NeuRoC Lab with Prof. Guillaume Durandau, which led to a first-author extended abstract accepted at ICNR 2026.
